  - `DomainException`

  - `InvalidArgumentException`

  - `LengthException`

  - `OutOfRangeException`

- `RuntimeException` (extends `Exception`)

  - `OutOfBoundsException`

  - `OverflowException`

  - `RangeException`

  - `UnderflowException`

  - `UnexpectedValueException`
