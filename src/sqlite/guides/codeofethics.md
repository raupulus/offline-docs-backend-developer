# 1. History

This document was originally called a "Code of Conduct" and was created for the purpose of filling in a box on "supplier registration" forms submitted to the SQLite developers by some clients. However, we subsequently learned that "Code of Conduct" has a very specific and almost sacred meaning to some readers, a meaning to which this document does not conform [\[1\]](https://www.theregister.co.uk/2018/10/22/sqlite_code_of_conduct/)[\[2\]](https://pjmedia.com/news-and-politics/paula-bolyard/2018/10/24/tech-community-outraged-after-sqlite-founder-adopts-christian-code-of-conduct-n61746)[\[3\]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S48VzyCwwtk). Therefore this document was renamed to "Code of Ethics", as we are encouraged to do by rule 71 in particular and also rules 2, 8, 9, 18, 19, 30, 66, and in the spirit of all the rest.

This document continues to be used for its original purpose - providing a reference to fill in the "code of conduct" box on supplier registration forms.

# 2. Purpose

The founder of SQLite, and all of the current developers at the time when this document was composed, have pledged to govern their interactions with each other, with their clients, and with the larger SQLite user community in accordance with the "instruments of good works" from chapter 4 of [The Rule of St. Benedict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rule_of_Saint_Benedict) (hereafter: "The Rule"). This code of ethics has proven its mettle in thousands of diverse communities for over 1,500 years, and has served as a baseline for many civil law codes since the time of Charlemagne.

## 2.1. Scope of Application

No one is required to follow The Rule, to know The Rule, or even to think that The Rule is a good idea. The Founder of SQLite believes that anyone who follows The Rule will live a happier and more productive life, but individuals are free to dispute or ignore that advice if they wish.

The founder of SQLite and all current developers have pledged to follow the spirit of The Rule to the best of their ability. They view The Rule as their promise to all SQLite users of how the developers are expected to behave. This is a one-way promise, or covenant. In other words, the developers are saying: "We will treat you this way regardless of how you treat us."

10. Do not do to another what you would not have done to yourself. <span id="r10"></span>
11. Deny oneself in order to follow Christ. <span id="r11"></span>
12. Chastise the body. <span id="r12"></span>
13. Do not become attached to pleasures. <span id="r13"></span>
14. Love fasting. <span id="r14"></span>
15. Relieve the poor. <span id="r15"></span>
16. Clothe the naked. <span id="r16"></span>
17. Visit the sick. <span id="r17"></span>
18. Bury the dead. <span id="r18"></span>
19. Be a help in times of trouble. <span id="r19"></span>
20. Console the sorrowing. <span id="r20"></span>
21. Be a stranger to the world's ways. <span id="r21"></span>
22. Prefer nothing more than the love of Christ. <span id="r22"></span>
23. Do not give way to anger. <span id="r23"></span>
24. Do not nurse a grudge. <span id="r24"></span>
25. Do not entertain deceit in your heart. <span id="r25"></span>
26. Do not give a false peace. <span id="r26"></span>
27. Do not forsake charity. <span id="r27"></span>
28. Do not swear, for fear of perjuring yourself. <span id="r28"></span>
29. Utter only truth from heart and mouth. <span id="r29"></span>
30. Do not return evil for evil. <span id="r30"></span>
31. Do no wrong to anyone, and bear patiently wrongs done to yourself. <span id="r31"></span>
32. Love your enemies. <span id="r32"></span>
33. Do not curse those who curse you, but rather bless them. <span id="r33"></span>
34. Bear persecution for justice's sake. <span id="r34"></span>
35. Be not proud. <span id="r35"></span>
36. Be not addicted to wine. <span id="r36"></span>
37. Be not a great eater. <span id="r37"></span>
38. Be not drowsy. <span id="r38"></span>
39. Be not lazy. <span id="r39"></span>
40. Be not a grumbler. <span id="r40"></span>
41. Be not a detractor. <span id="r41"></span>
42. Put your hope in God. <span id="r42"></span>
43. Attribute to God, and not to self, whatever good you see in yourself. <span id="r43"></span>
44. Recognize always that evil is your own doing, and to impute it to yourself. <span id="r44"></span>
45. Fear the Day of Judgment. <span id="r45"></span>
46. Be in dread of hell. <span id="r46"></span>
47. Desire eternal life with all the passion of the spirit. <span id="r47"></span>

49. Keep constant guard over the actions of your life. <span id="r49"></span>
50. Know for certain that God sees you everywhere. <span id="r50"></span>
51. When wrongful thoughts come into your heart, dash them against Christ immediately. <span id="r51"></span>

53. Guard your tongue against evil and depraved speech. <span id="r53"></span>
54. Do not love much talking. <span id="r54"></span>
55. Speak no useless words or words that move to laughter. <span id="r55"></span>
56. Do not love much or boisterous laughter. <span id="r56"></span>
57. Listen willingly to holy reading. <span id="r57"></span>
58. Devote yourself frequently to prayer. <span id="r58"></span>
59. Daily in your prayers, with tears and sighs, confess your past sins to God, and amend them for the future. <span id="r59"></span>
60. Fulfill not the desires of the flesh; hate your own will. <span id="r60"></span>
61. Obey in all things the commands of those whom God has placed in authority over you even though they (which God forbid) should act otherwise, mindful of the Lord's precept, "Do what they say, but not what they do." <span id="r61"></span>
62. Do not wish to be called holy before one is holy; but first to be holy, that you may be truly so called. <span id="r62"></span>
63. Fulfill God's commandments daily in your deeds. <span id="r63"></span>
64. Love chastity. <span id="r64"></span>
65. Hate no one. <span id="r65"></span>
66. Be not jealous, nor harbor envy. <span id="r66"></span>
67. Do not love quarreling. <span id="r67"></span>
68. Shun arrogance. <span id="r68"></span>
69. Respect your seniors. <span id="r69"></span>
70. Love your juniors. <span id="r70"></span>
71. Pray for your enemies in the love of Christ. <span id="r71"></span>
72. Make peace with your adversary before the sun sets. <span id="r72"></span>
73. Never despair of God's mercy.
