SQLite is written and maintained by a team of dedicated software professionals located around the world - on three continents.

This page used to host names and pictures of key developers. However, it turns out there are a lot of crazy people in the world who might misuse that information, so we have taken it down for now.

If you really need to know exactly who are the developers that bring you SQLite, please contact the lead developer (Richard Hipp) via email to drh at this domain so he can arrange appropriate introductions.
